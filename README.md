## 💫 About Me

Hey! I spend most of my time building AI agents and the apps around them, on whatever platform they need to run.

- 🤖 Lately it's been agents, RAG and MCP, mostly in Python and Rust
- 📱 I've shipped React Native and Flutter apps to both stores, and I reach for Tauri or Electron when something needs to live on the desktop
- 🌱 Picking up native iOS and Android with SwiftUI and Jetpack Compose, and digging into how LLMs work under the hood
- 🏍️ When I'm not coding, I'm probably thinking about bikes or planning the next solo trip
